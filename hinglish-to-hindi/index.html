How it works for Hinglish to Hindi

Converting Hinglish to Hindi is simple. The tool reads your romanized Hindi text, matches each word against a built-in dictionary of 150+ common words, and fills the gaps with phonetic transliteration rules. The result is accurate Devanagari you can copy and use anywhere.

This works for single words, full sentences, paragraphs and even long blocks of text. The scope selector lets you control how much context the converter uses when choosing the right Devanagari form.

  • kya → क्या
  • bahut → बहुत
  • achha → अच्छा
  • thik → ठीक
  • nahi → नहीं
  • kaise → कैसे
