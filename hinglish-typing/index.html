Hinglish typing

Learn how to type Hindi on a standard English keyboard — rules, mappings and practice sentences.

What is Hinglish typing?

Hinglish typing is the practice of writing Hindi using the English alphabet on a standard QWERTY keyboard. Instead of switching to a Devanagari keyboard layout, you type Hindi words phonetically using Roman letters — for example, "kya haal hai" instead of using a Hindi keyboard. This is how hundreds of millions of Indians type on phones and laptops every day, and it is the foundation of all Hinglish communication.

Basic Hinglish typing rules

Hinglish typing follows a simple principle: type what you hear. Hindi sounds are represented using English letters and letter combinations. Here are the most common mappings.

Tips for fast Hinglish typing

  • Use dictionary words. Stick to common Hindi words that are widely recognized — kya, kaise, bahut, thik, nahi. Fancy or rare Hindi words are harder to type and read in Roman script.
  • Type phonetically. Don't worry about exact Devanagari spelling rules. If your typing sounds like the Hindi word, other Hinglish readers will understand it. "Mujhe" and "mujhe" and "mujh" all work.
  • Use the converter to check. Type your Hinglish text in the converter below and switch to Hindi to see if your Devanagari output looks correct. This helps you learn the right spellings over time.
  • Shorten where natural. Hinglish speakers often drop formal markers. "Kya kar rahe ho?" becomes "Kya kar rahe?" and that is perfectly fine in informal text.
