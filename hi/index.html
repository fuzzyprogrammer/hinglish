Frequently asked questions

Hinglish is the everyday mix of Hindi and English used across India — for example "Mujhe nahi pata, honestly!" It blends Hindi words and grammar with English, written in the Roman alphabet.

In everyday use, absolutely — hundreds of millions speak it, and it dominates Indian advertising, film and social media. Linguists call it code-switching or a mixed variety, not a standardized language.
