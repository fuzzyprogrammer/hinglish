Everything you need to know about Hinglish — meaning, typing, conversion and more.

Hinglish is the everyday mix of Hindi and English used across India. It blends Hindi words and grammar with English vocabulary, written in the Roman alphabet. It is one of the fastest-growing informal communication styles in the world, dominating texting, social media and casual conversation among hundreds of millions of speakers.

Hinglish is the informal combination of Hindi and English — both languages mixed in the same sentence and written using the English alphabet. Instead of choosing one language, speakers naturally switch between both. Phrases like "Mujhe call karo later" or "The meeting was bahut boring" are everyday Hinglish.

Hinglish Hindi aur English ka mix hai jo India mein bahut popular hai. Log sentences mein dono languages ko Roman alphabet mein likhte hain — jaise "Mujhe nahi pata, honestly!" ya "Chal, movie dekhte hain." Ye texting, social media aur casual baatcheet mein sabse zyada use hota hai.

The word "Hinglish" is a combination of "Hindi" and "English." It refers to the communication style where both languages are blended in daily speech and writing, most commonly using Roman script. The term first appeared in Indian media in the late 1990s as this mixing became increasingly visible in advertising and television.

Hinglish ka matlab hai Hindi aur English ka mix. Ye ek informal communication style hai jisme dono languages ko Roman alphabet mein likha jaata hai. India mein ye texting aur social media par sabse zyada use hota hai — har din crores log isme baat karte hain.

Linguists classify Hinglish as code-switching — the practice of alternating between two languages within a sentence or conversation — rather than a separate standardized language with its own grammar. However, it is absolutely real in usage: hundreds of millions of people speak it daily, it dominates Indian digital communication, and many Hinglish words have entered formal dictionaries.

Yes. ChatGPT and most modern AI models are trained on large amounts of Hinglish text from the internet. They understand mixed Hindi-English sentences, can reply in Hinglish, translate between Hindi and English, and even help fix grammar in Hinglish text.

Reports commonly cite Hindi (and its Hinglish variants) as the language understood by roughly 42% of Indians, making it the most widely spoken language in the country. A large portion of that daily usage is actually Hinglish — the informal Hindi-English mix that dominates online communication, texting and casual speech across metro cities and small towns alike.

Hinglish typing means writing Hindi using the English alphabet on a standard QWERTY keyboard. You type Hindi words phonetically — for example "kya haal hai" — without needing a Devanagari keyboard. This is how most Hindi speakers type on phones and laptops, and it is the foundation of Hinglish communication.
